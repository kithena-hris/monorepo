Reach Mobile · Data

Table

On a phone, each row becomes a card. The first column is the title, the key values sit underneath it, and tapping the card opens the detail.

ColumnChooser

Lets people pick and reorder table columns. Their choice is saved for each table.

FilterBuilder

Build filters out of conditions. Applied filters then show as chips above the results.

Kanban

Move work between stages. Drag with a mouse or a finger, or use the keyboard or the card menu. Every drag has a non-drag alternative.

KeyValues

Label and value pairs for record details. Keys are quiet and values are clear.

Money

Money stays exact. It’s stored in minor units, formatted for the locale, and always shown with its currency.

Pagination

On a phone, pagination is "Page 3 of 52" between two arrows. Lists usually load more as you scroll instead.

SortableList

Reorder a short list by dragging. Keyboard and screen readers are supported, and every move is announced.

Stat

One number with context. Direction and sentiment are separate: fewer leavers is down, and that’s good.

VirtualList

Renders only the rows you can see, so 20,000 rows scroll smoothly. Row heights can vary.

Complex filters

For big lists: saved views, facets with counts, nested and/or groups, and a way back when nothing matches. Every filter is shown as a chip you can remove.

Tree view

Nested items such as folders, departments or cost centres. Arrow keys move through it, and branches load when you open them.

Scheduler

A day view with a strip of dates, plus an agenda list. On a phone, the week view only works in landscape.
