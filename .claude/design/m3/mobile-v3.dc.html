Kithena · Mobile app · People, Time off, Inbox, Settings

Kithena mobile

The phone app, rebuilt around one screen per tab. People, Time off and Inbox each open straight onto everything they hold; short tasks are centred modals; settings read as plain sentences, and Kithena drafts the change when you describe it. 60 screens across an employee (Adam), a manager (Marco), HR (Ada) and a new starter (Mei).

Decisions

Each decision, with what it replaces.

1
One screen per tabPeople opened a menu of Directory, Review, Import & export and Insights, each its own page.The People tab is the directory, with what needs you on top. Review is decided in place; import, export and insights sit in one ⋯ menu.
2
Profiles you can read at a glanceLabel and value in the same size and weight, one long column, pills to switch section.A photo header with quick actions and four key facts, then one card per section. Every row has an icon, a small grey label and a large value.
3
Centred modals, not sheetsBottom sheets for requests, filters, clock-outs and edits.Every short task is a centred modal with its buttons in reach. Only long flows (onboarding, parental leave) take a full screen.
4
Settings in plain sentencesMulti-step wizards and walls of checkboxes.Each setting reads as one sentence with tappable parts. Or describe the change: Kithena drafts it, shows exactly what moves, and nothing applies until you confirm.
5
AI where it saves a stepA separate assistant page.Search takes questions, requests can be described, imports are mapped, fields and leave types are drafted from a sentence, and each tab opens with a one-line summary. Ask Kithena stays a small card above the tab bar.
6
Act from the cardOpen every item to approve, sign or add.Tasks carry their main button on the card. Approve, Fix, Sign and Add happen in place; detail is one tap away.
